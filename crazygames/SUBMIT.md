# CrazyGames — данни за формата

Пакет: `npm run crazy` → `release/metko-tycoon-crazygames.zip` (index.html + engine.js, ~33 KB).
Корици: `npm run covers` (глобален Playwright: `PW="$(npm root -g)/playwright"`) → `crazygames/covers/`.
Езикът се избира сам: браузър на български → български, иначе английски. Ръчно: Статистика → Настройки → 🌐.

Първо пускане: Basic Launch (без SDK, без реклами). За Full Launch трябват CrazyGames SDK (gameplayStart/stop, реклами, Data модул за записа).

## Title
Metko Tycoon

## Short description (EN)
Build a money empire from a single lemonade stand all the way to a galactic empire. Hire managers, buy upgrades, sell your empire to investors and grow faster every run.

## Description (EN)
Metko Tycoon is a fast idle tycoon game. Start with one lemonade stand and grow into bakeries, pizzerias, hotels, football clubs, banks, oil companies, AI labs, space agencies and finally a Galactic Empire.

- 15 businesses, each faster and richer than the last
- Hire managers so every business runs by itself, even while you are away
- 224 upgrades that triple your profit
- Milestones: every 25, 50, 100… units make a business faster or more profitable
- Sell your empire to investors for a permanent profit bonus and start over stronger
- Golden briefcases, market frenzy, turbo boost and a daily bonus
- Diamond shop with permanent perks and time warps
- 117 achievements, each with a permanent +2% profit bonus
- Offline earnings, automatic saving, light and dark theme

## Controls (EN)
Mouse / touch: tap a business icon to earn money, tap Buy to add units, Hire for managers.
Choose ×1, ×10, ×100, NEXT (next milestone) or MAX in the Buy bar.
Tap the golden briefcase when it appears. Use Turbo for ×3 profit for 60 seconds.
